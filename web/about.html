Our Story

A Decade Building Park View's Most Trusted Property Brand

Unique Properties began as a single-window dealership in Park View Lahore — built on the simple idea that luxury real estate deserves honest service. A decade on, we are a boutique agency known for our deep block-level expertise, curated portfolio, and our long-term relationships with families, investors and overseas Pakistanis.

Every property we list goes through our verification process. Every client gets a dedicated agent. And every recommendation is grounded in real Park View market data — not pressure tactics.
